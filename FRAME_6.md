### FRAME 6: MANUFACTURED HARMONY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid urban photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a heavy silver halide noise.  
A massive, blindingly bright digital billboard  
Mounted on the weathered concrete wall of a towering, gray high-rise.  
On the screen — a young, beautiful woman in a perfect yoga pose  
Beneath a fake, glowing neon sun, promoting forced peace and toxic optimism.  

Below, the quiet winter street is drowning in a messy, melting gray slush,  
Dirty snow and dark puddles bleeding with artificial pink-orange reflections.  
A lonely city bus rolls blindly through the cold, overcast daylight.  
A cynical clashing of beautiful manufactured illusions  
And the raw, heavy weight of modern digital burnout.  
Fujichrome film style, deep cinematic contrast,  
The fragile vulnerability of a crumbling world.
