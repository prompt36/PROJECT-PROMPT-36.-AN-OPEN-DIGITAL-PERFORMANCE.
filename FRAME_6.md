### FRAME 6: CHEMICAL COMFORT
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A stark, high-contrast urban documentary photograph,  
Raw 35mm analog film style with a heavy film grain texture.  
A massive, blindingly bright digital advertising billboard,  
Mounted on the weathered concrete wall of a grim, gray panel-block high-rise.  
The billboard displays a vibrant commercial with a family,  
Showing unnaturally perfect, forced smiles, promoting anti-anxiety medicine.  

The depressing street below is covered in messy, melting gray slush,  
Dirty winter snow, mud, and water puddles reflecting the artificial neon glow.  
Flat, overcast daylight creating deep heavy contrast,  
Muted real-world color palette vs the blinding artificial light.  

Fujichrome film grading with fine silver halide noise,  
A melancholic and cynical urban landscape of digital burnout,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw atmospheric realism,  
A chemical shield against the modern world.
