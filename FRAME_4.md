### FRAME 4: THE SILENT SMART-HOME
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, candid indoor photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with rich silver halide noise.  
The interior of an ultra-modern, expensive minimalist apartment  
Completely dead, dark, and blacked out during a sudden power outage.  
Sleek high-tech gadgets and blank biometric screens are cold.  

In the center of the pitch-black luxury room,  
A lonely human is sitting flat on the hard floor,  
Hands dimly illuminated by the small, flickering orange flame of a single match.  
The faint warm light carves an anxious face out of the heavy shadows.  

High-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, deep graphic darkness.  
Muted real-world color palette vs a tiny spark of fire.  
Fujichrome film style with deep cinematic film grain texture.  
A terrifying cage of frozen code and dead electricity,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture,  
The fragile vulnerability of a digital kingdom.
