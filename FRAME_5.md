### FRAME 5: ECO-HYPOCRISY
### THE MANTRA-CODE:

A raw, wide-angle documentary street photograph,  
Grainy 35mm analog film style with a gritty reportage look.  
A crowd of trendy, fashionable young people at an eco-protest rally,  
Standing in a sunlit city square with premium smartphones in their hands.  
They are holding neatly printed cardboard signs with environmental slogans,  
Their eyes locked onto glowing screens instead of the world.  

In the immediate foreground, the concrete ground is heavily cluttered,  
Strewn with abandoned single-use plastic cups, crushed disposable vapes,  
And littered brand-name wrappers left by the activists.  
Harsh natural daylight casting deep, unforgiving shadows.  
Real-world dirt, trash texture, and organic imperfections.  

Fujichrome film grading with a fine silver halide noise,  
A cynical snapshot of performative awareness and digital greenwashing,  
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw analog texture.
