FRAME 1: BORDER OF WORLDS
THE MANTRA-CODE:

A cinematic wide shot in 35mm film grain style,
Inspired by the raw aesthetics of Hasselblad XPan.
A massive, towering neon 3D advertising screen in Tokyo at dusk,
Broadcasting a hyper-realistic, pristine, glowing AI-generated avatar
With an unnaturally perfect, frozen smile.
	
Directly below the blinding screen, in a deep, stark shadow,
On the wet, dark, light-reflective asphalt,
A single real human is walking completely alone.
Dressed in a dark oversized jacket, face dimly lit from below
By the small cold screen of a smartphone.
	
Harsh graphical lighting and heavy moody contrast.
Muted real-world tones clashing with artificial neon light.
Fujichrome color grading, fine silver halide noise,
Documentary street photography, atmospheric realism, 
No 3D-render smoothness, only raw texture.
